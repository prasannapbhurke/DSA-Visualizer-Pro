Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026 • Effective Date: September 13, 2026

01. Introduction

Welcome to DSA Visualizer Pro ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide an interactive visual learning platform and algorithm practice suite designed to help students, developers, and educators explore Data Structures and Algorithms through dynamic step-by-step animations and quizzes.

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how that information is utilized, protected, and shared, and the specific rights you maintain regarding your personal information, including account and data deletion.

02. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to deliver core educational experiences and cloud synchronization:

  • Account & Profile Information (via Google Sign-In): When you choose to sign in to sync your progress, we receive basic profile information provided by Google OAuth 2.0 with your explicit consent: your Name, Email address, Google Profile Picture URL, and your unique Google Account ID. We never request or access your Google account password.
  • Educational Progress & Learning Metrics: We collect your interactive visualization usage, quiz completion scores, algorithm mastery levels, daily learning streaks, time elapsed per quiz session, and questions saved to your Mistake Bank.
  • Classroom Collaboration Data: If you join or host a virtual classroom, we store enrollment associations, join codes, and teacher evaluation notes associated with your user ID.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Minimal non-identifiable technical data such as API response timestamps and error stack traces to troubleshoot and maintain backend uptime. We do not collect precision GPS location data, contacts, SMS, or biometric identifiers.

03. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Authenticating your user session securely across devices.
  • Synchronizing your learning progress, quiz attempts, and streaks between mobile and web.
  • Personalizing your algorithm dashboard (identifying weak topics and suggesting recommended visualizations).
  • Preventing fraudulent abuse or automated Denial of Service (DoS) attacks on our API.
Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information or learning activity to third parties or advertising networks.

04. Third-Party Services

To deliver our mobile and cloud experience, we integrate with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers that adhere to rigorous security standards:

  • Google Play Services & Google Identity: Used to facilitate secure, single-tap Google Sign-In authentication.
  • Supabase & PostgreSQL: Used for secure, encrypted cloud persistence of your learning progress and classrooms.
  • Vercel Cloud Platform: Hosts our application programming interface (API) with automated SSL/TLS encryption.

05. Data Security & Storage

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All network communications between the DSA Visualizer Pro mobile application and our backend servers are strictly enforced using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.

06. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

We retain your information only as long as you maintain an active account with DSA Visualizer Pro. You hold the absolute right to have all your personal information, quiz history, and progress permanently deleted at any time.

Right to Erasure (Google Play Requirement): You can request full account deletion via either of two straightforward paths:
  • In-App Deletion: Open the DSA Visualizer Pro mobile application → Tap the Navigation Drawer menu → Tap "Delete Account" → Confirm deletion.
  • Web Self-Service Deletion Portal: You can submit a deletion request online at any time without needing the app installed:

Open Web Account Deletion Portal →

Upon initiation, your profile, Google identifier, quiz sessions, mistake records, and classroom enrollments are immediately and irreversibly erased from our databases.

07. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so that we can promptly delete the data.

08. Policy Updates

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
