Delete Account & Data

You have full control over your personal data. Fulfilling Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you can permanently delete your DSA Visualizer Pro account and associated records at any time.

× What Happens When You Delete Your Account

Upon submitting a deletion request, the following data is permanently and irreversibly purged from our cloud databases:

  • Account Profile: Your Google ID, name, email address, profile avatar, and authentication tokens.
  • Learning Progress: All recorded algorithm topic completions, best quiz scores, mastery levels, and last-viewed timestamps.
  • Practice & Quizzes: Complete history of practice sessions, quiz attempts, timing, and scores.
  • Mistake Bank: All saved incorrect practice questions and notes.
  • Classrooms: Any student enrollments, teacher notes, or hosted classroom records associated with your account.

Data Retention: Zero days. We do not maintain delayed retention or soft-delete holding periods once a deletion request is executed. All associated records are removed immediately.

→ Web Self-Service Deletion

Enter the Google email address associated with your DSA Visualizer Pro account. We will look up and permanently purge all matching account data.

Deletion Complete: If an account exists with this email, it and all associated progress and quiz data have been permanently deleted from our database.

📱 Delete From Within The Mobile App

You can also delete your account instantly from within the Android application:

1. Open DSA Visualizer Pro on your Android device.
2. Tap the Menu icon (≡) in the top left to open the Navigation Drawer.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account".
4. Confirm the prompt "Delete Permanently". Your cloud data and local cached sessions will be wiped immediately.
